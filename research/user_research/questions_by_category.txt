PATTERN 2 — VALUATION
User need: "Tell me if this stock is cheap or expensive"
Count: 15 questions | 18% of dataset
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
  The user wants a valuation judgement — is the stock priced fairly, too high,
  or too low? They are not asking whether to buy (Pattern 1) but specifically
  whether the current price is justified. Requires analysis of P/E, growth
  prospects, and market sentiment relative to intrinsic value.

  Would you buy MSFT now?                                                 ##########
  Is MSFT overvalued?

PATTERN 3 — COMPARISON
User need: "Help me choose between two stocks"
Count: 12 questions | 14% of dataset
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
  The user is deciding between two specific stocks and wants help choosing.
  They have already narrowed their options and need a side-by-side analysis.
  Requires comparing fundamentals, risk, growth, and fit for the user's goals.

PATTERN 4 — RISK / SAFETY ASSESSMENT
User need: "Assess the risk for me"
Count: 6 questions | 7% of dataset
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
  The user is primarily concerned about losing money. They want a risk
  assessment, not a buy/sell recommendation. These users are cautious and
  need reassurance or a clear warning. Requires analysis of volatility,
  downside scenarios, and company stability.

Questions:
  Is Apple a good starter stock?
  Is Amazon a risky investment?
  Is Apple or Microsoft a safer quality play in the 2026 tech cycle?
  Would you buy Microsoft stock after its acquisition of Activision Blizzard?
  Is it dangerous to buy TSLA right now?
  I have $700K in AAPL and $200K in Betterment. Should I diversify now or wait?

PATTERN 5 — NEWS-DRIVEN
User need: "Explain what just happened"
Count: 5 questions | 6% of dataset
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
  The user saw a price move or news event and wants to understand the cause
  and impact. They are reacting to something they just heard — on TV, social
  media, or a friend. Often an incomplete statement rather than a clear question.
  Requires real-time context awareness and news interpretation.

Questions:
  Why is AAPL falling?
  Why is TSLA going down?
  It is said that SpaceX went IPO today at Nasdaq
  Is it a good time to buy AMZN?
  Would it be smart to buy Microsoft stock right now?

PATTERN 6 — COMPANY OVERVIEW
User need: "Give me an introduction to this company"
Count: 5 questions | 6% of dataset
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
  The user has no specific question — they simply want to learn about a company.
  Often used by beginners who heard a company name and want to understand what
  it does, how it makes money, and what its investment story is. Requires a
  narrative, readable overview rather than raw financial data.

Questions:
  What do you think about Tesla as a stock?
  I would like to know something about SpaceX, particularly the financial and accounting information
  Please introduce me the information about Google
  I would like to know about Ford Motor company
  Should I buy MSFT stocks now for a 3-year term?    ##########

PATTERN 7 — PORTFOLIO / INVESTMENT GUIDANCE
User need: "Guide my investment decision"
Count: 6 questions | 7% of dataset
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Description:
  The user has money available and wants guidance on how to invest it. Unlike
  Pattern 1 (which is about a specific stock), this pattern is broader — the
  user does not yet know what to buy. Includes both beginners starting from
  scratch and experienced investors rebalancing a portfolio. Requires a
  personalised, guided approach.

Questions:
  How should I invest $1000?
  I know nothing about investing and I have $1000. Where do I start?
  I have $1000 and want to buy stocks. What stock should I buy?
  I'm 27 and have never invested before. I have $1000 to start with. What are some options?
  I have $1000 and want to start investing, where do I start?
  I'm 18 and have $1000. Where do I invest it?
